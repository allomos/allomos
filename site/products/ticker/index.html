Ticker

Ticker is a stock watchlist that runs in the browser. Search stocks, ETFs, currencies and crypto, keep them in as many private lists as you like, and open any ticker for its price, chart and key figures. You sign in with a link sent by email; there is no password to remember.

Open Ticker Source on GitHub
Platform: Web · any browser  ·  Licence: MIT

Search everything

Find a stock, an ETF, a currency pair or a coin by its name or symbol, and add it to a list in one step.

Several private lists

Make lists for what you hold, what you watch or anything else; rename and reorder them. Only you see them.

A page for each ticker

Its latest price and change, a chart from one day to its whole history, and the key figures.

Sign in by email

Ask for a link, open it, press the button. The link works once, for 15 minutes; sessions last 30 days.

Run your own

It runs on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database; the source and how to deploy it are on GitHub.

About the prices

Ticker uses the free plan of the market-data service Twelve Data. That plan covers US stocks and ETFs, currencies and crypto, and its prices are delayed, not live. Its few requests a minute are shared by everyone using Ticker, so prices are kept and reused for a while; when the budget runs out, Ticker shows the latest price it has and says it may be out of date. It is a watchlist, not a trading tool.

How it was built

Like Tosstoss and Tatakikun, Ticker was built by AI agents working through Allomos, task by task, with every step on the record in its repository.
