Released 2026-11-03. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.38).

## New

- **A group's Edit sits on its title line.** In the task list, each area and iteration group's **Edit** now comes right after the group's name (and an iteration's state badge), instead of on the row under the progress bar; **New task** stays on that lower row. Edit opens the same form as before and never folds or unfolds the group. **Unplanned** and a group with an unknown area still have no Edit.
- **State words in normal weight.** The state of a task, in its row, in the details panel and in the **Show** filter, is no longer bold. It keeps its colour, and its light tint where it had one, in light and dark mode.
