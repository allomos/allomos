Released 2026-11-16. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.64).

## New

- **Monitors move to the new version after an update.** When Allomos restarts to update, a running **Monitor ready tasks** no longer keeps going on the old version's `alm`: the app stops it, starts it again with the new version, and keeps its settings. A notification and a line in the status bar's monitor panel say so, and the reminder before **Restart to Update** mentions it too. Monitors run from a source build or another `alm` are left alone.

## Fixed

- **A monitor that cannot read its taskbag tells you.** If the taskbag's format needs a newer Allomos, you are told at once; if the taskbag cannot be read for 2 minutes or more, you are told then. You get a notification, a warning line next to the monitor in the status bar, and a ⚠ on the head of the **Auto-monitor** section (folded or open). The monitor keeps trying every few seconds and carries on by itself once it can read the taskbag again; the warning then goes away.
