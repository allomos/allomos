Released 2026-11-20. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.79).

## New

- **Approve several tasks at once.** `alm checked` and `alm reviewed` take several task IDs. If any one of them cannot be recorded, none is, and `alm` says which one and why. A failed check now points to `alm reopen`.
- **A clearer notice about unshared changes.** Changes made directly in the app are grouped together, with repeated edits of the same item merged, and a short line explains why the notice appears and what asking an agent to share does.

## Fixed

- **The monitor stops showing sessions that already moved on.** A task that was given out again or finished no longer stays under “did not start”, and times from another day show their date.
