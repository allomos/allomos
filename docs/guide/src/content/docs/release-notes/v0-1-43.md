Released 2026-11-08. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.43).

## New

- **The history says who, for whom, under which approval and why.** Every change now records who made it: a person by a pseudonym, or an agent by its session, model, effort, permission mode, rules version and `alm` version. It also records whom it was done for and which approval it used. `alm --why "<reason>"` adds the reason; when a state change or a change of plan has none, `alm` says so.
- **Approvals are records.** `alm approve` records who approved which plan, when, where and until when; `alm withdraw` takes one back. The task pane has an **Approvals** section that shows whether each still applies, or has expired, been withdrawn, or no longer matches because the description changed. **Approve…** shows the plan exactly as written before you approve it, a task in backlog also offers **Approve and make ready**, and **Withdraw…** asks why. Approving does not change a task's state.
- **A human check records who, against which steps, and how it went.** **Checked** and `alm checked` record the checker, which version of the check steps they followed, the result (`--result pass` or `partly`) and their words (`--words`).
- **Exceptions are written down.** A check that was not run, or a task finished without the human check, is recorded with the reason and who accepted the risk: `alm exception`, listed with `alm exceptions`. `alm done --without-human-check` records its own.
- **Change type and risk.** `alm classify` records a task's change type (standard, normal or emergency), its risk and what it touches. An emergency change is listed in `alm human` under **To approve afterwards** until someone approves it.
- **Tasks link to code, releases, requirements and decisions.** `alm link` records a task's commits, branches, merges, pull requests, CI runs and releases, and `alm links` finds the task from any of them; the task pane shows them under **Code and releases**. `alm trace` links a task to requirement IDs and decision records, and `alm traces` finds the tasks for one.
- **A ledger of outside effects.** `alm effect` records each outside effect before it happens (where, what, how to undo it, and under which approval) and its result afterwards; `alm effects` lists them, and the task pane shows them under **Outside effects**.
- **A signed history and alm audit.** In the new taskbag format `allomos/8`, each computer signs the events it writes with a key of its own (kept in the macOS keychain) and chains them. `alm checkpoint` saves a signed fingerprint of the taskbag, and `alm audit` lists any event that was changed, removed or never signed. New taskbags start in `allomos/8`. A taskbag in `allomos/7` keeps working as it is, unsigned, until you run `alm upgrade`. After the upgrade, Allomos 0.1.42 and older can no longer write to it, so install 0.1.43 on every computer that works on it first.
- **Project settings → Audit.** Four settings, each change recorded with a reason. **Identity**: the history holds only a pseudonym; an Allomos account and company sign-in (SSO) need a cloud project and are not available yet. **Retention**: how long the history is kept; Allomos never deletes anything on its own. **Legal hold**: while it is on, nothing can be removed. **Separation of duties**: off by default; when it is on, the same person or agent cannot both do and check the same task. `alm redact` removes the past values of fields from a task's history, leaving a record that it did so but not the old values; it is refused during a legal hold.
- **Monitored terminal tabs close themselves (macOS).** When a monitored task is done, its Terminal tab says it closes in 30 seconds; press any key to keep it open. On Windows, monitored windows stay open as before.

## Also

- **The Show filter has a close button.** The **×** at the right of its tabs closes the panel and keeps the filter as it is.
