Released 2026-11-16. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.65).

## New

- **Each task shows how risky its change is.** The task list and the task pane show **High**, **Medium** or **Low**, as the agent that did the work recorded it with `alm classify`, with its one-line reason. A task no agent has rated shows **Not classified**; Allomos never guesses. `alm human` shows the risk too.
- **A Risk management section in the project settings.** It lists the fixed rules that make a change high risk, and lets you add, change and remove your project's own rules. Two limits for high-risk work are kept here too; both are off by default.
- **Change a task's difficulty from the list.** In an open row of the task list, click the difficulty (for example "4 · Hard") and choose a level from 1 to 5. Esc or a click outside closes it without changing anything.
- **`alm human` lists one task per line, in order.** Agents waiting on you come first (the one holding back more tasks, then the one waiting longest); then finished work to check, higher risk and longer wait first; last, tasks of an earlier iteration.

## Fixed

- **The window no longer freezes while the taskbag is read or written.** Opening a taskbag, saving a change, reading a task's history and the other taskbag and settings actions now run in the background, so the window stays responsive.

## Also

- Agents now record each task's risk before they finish it, and write handoff notes, AI decisions and check steps with one plain sentence for you first, the technical detail last.
