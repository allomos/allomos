Released 2026-11-02. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.33).

## New

- **Each task in the task list shows its facts like the details pane**: a small grey name before each value, two facts to a line, instead of one long grey sentence. Clicking the holder opens its session.
- **Every state has a colour of its own**, so ready, in progress, waiting, blocked and done are told apart at a glance. A row shows a label only when the task has one.
- **The welcome page and the About window say what Allomos is for: the work always carries on**, whoever takes over. The paragraph under the headline says when you switch: an agent or account hits its limit, or a teammate hands you a task.

## Fixed

- **When an agent asks you a question, the progress box says "Waiting for your reply"** with the question, instead of warning after 10 minutes that the agent may have stopped. Once you answer, it shows what the agent is doing again.
- **An agent that changes tasks with `alm` counts as still working**, so the progress box no longer says it has not reported for 10 minutes while it claims, labels and finishes tasks.
- **When an agent really goes quiet for 10 minutes**, the warning says it may be waiting for your reply in its window, or it may have stopped.
