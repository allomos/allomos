Released 2026-10-22. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.17).

## New

- **The sort menu works in the By area tab too.** It orders the tasks inside every area, and the same choice applies to both tabs, so you set it once.
- **Recently changed tasks come first.** Until you choose a sort, the task list shows the tasks changed most recently at the top.
- **New windows open at a comfortable size.** A window takes about 80 % of the width and 85 % of the height of the screen it opens on (between 720 × 480 and 1280 × 900) and is centred.

## Fixed

- **The start page looks balanced in any window.** The card fills the height, its content is centred and the margins are an even 20 px. In very tall windows it stops growing instead of stretching.
- **The task pane keeps its proportion when you resize the window.** Its width is remembered as a share of the list-and-pane area, not as a fixed number of pixels.
