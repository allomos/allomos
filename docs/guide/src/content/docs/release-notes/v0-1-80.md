Released 2026-11-20. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.80).

## New

- **Approve several tasks at once in For You.** Tick the tasks you want, or use Select all, and approve them together after one confirmation. A task that cannot be approved yet cannot be ticked, and says why.
- **Send a task back with a reason.** At the bottom of the Evidence screen, and in the pane of a finished task, **Send back…** asks for a reason and returns the task to ready. In the desktop app you can attach a screenshot of the screen. Each AI decision has **Disagree…**, which starts the reason with that decision's text.
- **Sent-back tasks show their reasons first.** `alm next`, `alm show` and `alm claim` list who sent the task back, when and what they wrote, ahead of the handoff note, newest first. When the monitor opens a session for a sent-back task, it tells the agent to read the reasons first.
- **Sent-back tasks go out first.** Within an iteration, `alm next` and the monitor put sent-back tasks ahead of the others.
