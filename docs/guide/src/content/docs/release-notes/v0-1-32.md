Released 2026-10-30. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.32).

## New

- **Dragging a tab feels like Chrome.** The tab moves with the pointer, and the other tabs open a gap where it will land; a tab trades places with its neighbour once it covers more than half of it, and the **+** button moves aside too.
- **Dragging a tab to another window is easier.** Let it go anywhere over the other window, not only over its tab bar. That window comes to the top, keeps its own tabs in view, and shows the tab in the gap where it will land, with its taskbag already showing.
- **Tabs that are not in front have a light gray frame**, so the front tab is easier to tell apart.

## Fixed

- **A dragged tab no longer jumps** between a Retina display and a standard one on the same Mac.
- **On macOS, a window no longer jumps to the top-left corner** of the screen while its tab is dragged over another window's tab bar.
