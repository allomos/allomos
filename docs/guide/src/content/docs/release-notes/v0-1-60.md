Released 2026-11-15. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.60).

## New

- **The task pane puts what matters first.** Depending on a task's state, the pane opens with its check steps, open question, blockers, handoff note, or description, while all fields, the full description, and the history fold away.
- **Labels and states follow the task language.** Fixed terms such as labels and states appear in the project's task language in the app and in `alm human`.
- **Cleaner project header.** The AI agent status line (quota chips, connection status, and unshared-changes note) is removed from the top of the project window.

## Fixed

- **Confirming an input-method word no longer saves forms.** Pressing Enter to pick a Chinese or Japanese candidate no longer submits forms such as adding a task.

## Also

- **Updated tagline.** The app welcome screen, About window, website, guide home, and READMEs now show the updated tagline in English, Chinese, and Japanese.
- Decisions 0024 and 0025 record switching without tying to one agent and keeping task records readable for people first.
