Released 2026-11-20. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.76).

## New

- **Review evidence in one place.** Tasks waiting for your check show an Evidence panel at the top of the task pane and in For You. It brings together the risk and what to do, check steps, a change summary, a link to test evidence, AI decisions to review, and a collapsible handoff.
- **See what changed in code.** The change summary shows the first line of each recorded commit or merge, followed by the files changed and lines added or removed. It reads your local Git history; a commit missing from this computer keeps its link with a clear message.
