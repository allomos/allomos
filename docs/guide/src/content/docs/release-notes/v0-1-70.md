Released 2026-11-17. [Download from GitHub](https://github.com/allomos/allomos/releases/tag/v0.1.70).

## New

- **A For You window lists what waits for you.** Open it from the **For You** button at the top right of the main window, which shows how many tasks wait, or from **View → For You** (⇧⌘Y). The left side lists the tasks to check, answer and review in order; the right side says what you need to do and shows the task. A task you finish leaves the list and the next one is selected.
- **Task names in the monitor panel open their details.** Under the sessions the monitor opened, click a task's name to show that task in the details pane; **Open session** still opens the session.

## Also

- **The app and `alm` were reorganized inside.** Their code is split into smaller parts; nothing you use changes.
