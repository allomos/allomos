Nested Show filter (your design), next to A

In this design, the Custom tab is one row per state, with that state's label ticks across the row. A task is listed when it matches any tick in its state's row. The tick before each state just sums up its row: ☑ all, ▣ some, ☐ none. The two “by human” labels are one column, Checked by a human, and Waiting has an extra column, Waits for me (whom alm wait --who names).
